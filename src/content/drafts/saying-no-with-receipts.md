Working notes:

- Thesis: the problem with most roadmap "no"s isn't the answer, it's the opacity. People accept losing a fair contest; they don't accept a black box.
- The receipt: when we defer an ask, the requester sees exactly what outranked it and by what score — the master list is readable by everyone.
- Effects observed: fewer relitigated decisions, escalations arrive pre-argued in the framework's terms, and "can you re-score this?" replaced "this is urgent."
- The hard parts: keeping scores honest under pressure, handling the exec override gracefully (log it as an override, don't retro-fit the score).
- Short, punchy post — this one should stay under 800 words.
