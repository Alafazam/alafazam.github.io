Working notes:

- Setup: what I've learned building the PM team at Increff — which interview signals correlated with performance a year later.
- Signals that survived: how candidates handle being wrong mid-interview; whether they ask about constraints before proposing solutions; specificity of their failure stories; whether their "impact" survives two follow-up questions.
- Signals that didn't: polished case-study frameworks, confident market-sizing, familiarity with the trendy stack of the year.
- Structural ideas: the "one decision, three depths" question; giving real (anonymized) roadmap trade-offs instead of hypotheticals.
- Caveat section: sample size honesty, survivorship bias in my own data.
