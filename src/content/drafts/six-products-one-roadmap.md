Working notes:

- The trap: six products each with a "fair share" of capacity produces six mediocre roadmaps and zero strategy.
- The shift: plan the portfolio, not the products — one master list (the ROA machinery), explicit bets ranked across product lines, and seasons where some products deliberately get nothing.
- How to say "your product gets maintenance-only this quarter" and keep the team and its stakeholders functional.
- Mechanics: shared capacity pool vs. dedicated pods, the quarterly rebalance ritual, how platform/infra work competes fairly with feature work.
- Connects to: Return on Attention (the scoring), Two Clocks (the delivery side).
