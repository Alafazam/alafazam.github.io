Working notes:

- Thesis: merchandising decisions (what to stock, where, at what depth) are recommendation problems with harsher constraints — inventory is finite, decisions are batched, and errors are expensive and slow to observe.
- Contrast with consumer recsys: instant feedback loops vs. season-long feedback loops; implicit signals vs. committed capital.
- What transfers: cold-start thinking (new stores/new styles), exploration vs. exploitation (range testing), the danger of optimizing to last season's demand.
- What doesn't: you can't A/B a buy. Simulation and counterfactuals do the work experiments can't.
- Ground it with 1–2 concrete examples from the Increff platform (anonymized numbers).
